THE ENTERPRISE CHALLENGE

Business links need more than shortening.

Consumer URL shorteners treat links as static redirects. In business environments, a shared URL points to high-value assets, contractual agreements, financial invoices, or sensitive systems. Once distributed, businesses lose control unless the link itself is programmable.

Once a business distributes a link through email, CRM, automated invoicing, or support desks, critical governance requirements emerge:

  • • Expire access: Automatically invalidate links when an offer, contract window, or session deadline concludes.
  • • Revoke access: Immediately terminate access across all distributed channels when a document is revised or cancelled.
  • • Limit usage: Enforce strict single-use or count-capped access for quotes, vouchers, and private downloads.
  • • Control who can open it: Restrict opening permissions by corporate email domain, geographic region, or verification policies.
  • • Change the destination: Update upstream target URLs without breaking links already printed or emailed to clients.
  • • Understand who accessed it: Obtain forensic audit trails, access timestamps, user-agent telemetry, and policy evaluations.
