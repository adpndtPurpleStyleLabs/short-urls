Add Custom Domain

Connect your domain or subdomain to use your own branding for short links.

You can use a subdomain (e.g. links.yourbrand.com) or a root domain (e.g. yourbrand.com).

What's next?

1 We'll show you the DNS record to add.
2 Add the record in your domain provider.
3 Verify and start using your domain.
